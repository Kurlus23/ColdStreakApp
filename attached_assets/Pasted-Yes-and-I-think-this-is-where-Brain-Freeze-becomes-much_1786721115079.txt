Yes — and I think this is where Brain Freeze becomes much more than a trivia game.

You'd be collecting something genuinely interesting: the same person's cognitive performance under different conditions, over time.

But I'd be careful with the wording "how the cold affects their brain." The app shouldn't claim that a trivia score proves a physiological effect. Instead, it can show how their performance changes during their own plunges versus their normal play.

The data you'd naturally collect

For every question:

Correct / incorrect
Response time
Points earned
Question difficulty
Category
Whether they were plunging
Water temperature
Time into plunge
Heart rate, if available
Benefit goal/stage
Plunge streak
Time/date

That gives you a surprisingly rich dataset without asking the user to do anything extra.

Then give them a "Brain Freeze" profile

After enough data:

Your trivia performance

	In the Cold	Outside Cold

Then:

Your cold performance

You answer 5% more questions correctly during your plunges.

Your average response time is 0.9 seconds faster in Cold Mode.

That's really compelling.

And if the data goes the other direction:

Interesting finding

Your accuracy is similar in and out of the cold, but your response time increases during longer plunges.

That's still useful.

The app doesn't need to tell the user what the result means. It can let them discover their pattern.
