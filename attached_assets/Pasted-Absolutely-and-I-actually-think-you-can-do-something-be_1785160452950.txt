Make it personalized

Age and weight shouldn't drastically change where the milestones occur because the evidence doesn't support exact formulas. However, they can influence how quickly someone cools.

A simple adjustment could work well:

Lean (BMI <22): milestones occur ~10–15% sooner.
Average BMI (22–28): default.
Higher BMI (>30): milestones occur ~10–20% later.

Age could have only a small effect:

Under 40: no adjustment.
40–60: +5%.
Over 60: +10%.

This keeps the personalization subtle and scientifically defensible.
