Speed of the answer and the correct answer should award points.  Even if you answer wrong but answer quickly you get points

Yes — that's better game design than making wrong = zero. It introduces a second skill: knowing the answer and making a decision quickly.

I'd make the score for each question have three pieces:

🧠 Brain Freeze Question Score

Base value × accuracy bonus × speed bonus

But I'd actually keep the math visually simple for the player.

Say every question is worth 100 points.

Correct answer:

Answer immediately → 100
Answer halfway through → 75
Answer near the deadline → 55

Wrong answer:

Answer immediately → 25
Answer halfway through → 15
Answer at the last second → 5

So even:

❌ Wrong — but you knew enough to take a shot quickly
+23

That is psychologically much better than:

❌ Wrong
0

You're rewarding engagement and decisiveness, not just knowledge.

But I'd add a "Perfect Answer" bonus

If they answer correctly and quickly, they get something special:

⚡ PERFECT! +125

That becomes the thing people chase.

For example:

Result	Score
Correct + instant	125
Correct + fast	110
Correct + normal	90
Correct + slow	60
Wrong + instant	25
Wrong + fast	20
Wrong + normal	10
Wrong + barely answered	5
No answer	0

The exact numbers can be tuned later. The important concept is:

Every question is an opportunity to score.

And this makes the 30-second timer really useful

Imagine the question appears:

00:00

🧠 Which organ consumes the most oxygen at rest?

A. Heart
B. Brain
C. Liver
D. Lungs

A little visual timer starts:

15... 14... 13...

They tap B at 3 seconds.

⚡ PERFECT ANSWER

+125

Then:

Next question in 27 seconds

Now they aren't just waiting for the next question.

They're thinking:

"Can I get another Perfect?"

This also makes streaks much more interesting

I'd actually change our earlier streak concept slightly.

Instead of simply:

5 correct answers = streak

I'd have:

🔥 Brain Streak

Correct answers increase it.

Fast correct answers increase it faster.

For example:

Correct + fast → 🔥🔥
Correct + slow → 🔥
Wrong → streak drops

So you could get:

Q1: Correct fast → 🔥🔥
Q2: Correct fast → 🔥🔥🔥🔥
Q3: Correct slow → 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥
Q4: Wrong fast → 🔥
Q5: Correct fast → 🔥🔥🔥

You didn't completely lose everything.

That gives the player a reason to recover from a wrong answer.

Now combine that with the plunge streak

This is where your overall scoring system starts getting really elegant.

Your final Brain Freeze score could essentially be:

Trivia performance
× Cold progression
× Plunge streak

So:

Kevin — Day 1

Trivia: 1,000
Cold: ×1.5
Streak: ×1.0

= 1,500

Kevin — Day 21

Same exact trivia performance:

Trivia: 1,000
Cold: ×1.5
Streak: ×2.0

= 3,000

But another player can beat Kevin if they're better at Brain Freeze.

That's important.

The streak gives you progression, but skill still wins the leaderboard.

And there's one really cool thing about this system

Speed becomes a risk/reward decision.

You know the answer?

Tap it immediately.

Not sure?

You have a few seconds to think.

Think too long and the potential score drops.

But if you genuinely don't know:

Take a guess.

You might still get points.

That feels much more like a game than a trivia quiz.

And it fits the cold plunge beautifully because you're sitting there with a finite amount of time. The question isn't simply:

"Do you know the answer?"

It's:

"How quickly can you figure it out?"

That's where Brain Freeze starts feeling like an actual game rather than trivia bolted onto a timer.
