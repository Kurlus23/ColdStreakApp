Cold Response & Physiology

9. What is the cold-shock response?

10. Which system strongly influences the cold-shock response?
A. Autonomic nervous system
B. Digestive system
C. Skeletal system
D. Reproductive system
✅ Answer: A — Autonomic nervous system

These acute responses—including vasoconstriction, shivering, increased ventilation, and cardiovascular changes—are well established features of human cold exposure.
