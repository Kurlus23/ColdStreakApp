🧊 Brain Freeze — 50 Cold Plunge Questions + Cold Takes
1. What causes shivering during cold exposure?

A. Rapid muscle contractions
B. Faster digestion
C. Increased sweating
D. Slower breathing

Answer: A — Rapid muscle contractions

🧊 COLD TAKE: Shivering uses rapid muscle contractions to generate heat.

2. What happens to skin blood vessels in cold?

A. They widen
B. They constrict
C. They disappear
D. They rupture

Answer: B — They constrict

🧊 COLD TAKE: Vasoconstriction reduces blood flow near the skin and limits heat loss.

3. What is cold-induced vasoconstriction designed to reduce?

A. Heat loss
B. Oxygen intake
C. Muscle growth
D. Blood sugar

Answer: A — Heat loss

🧊 COLD TAKE: Narrowing surface blood vessels helps conserve core heat.

4. What is shivering primarily used for?

A. Generating heat
B. Cooling the body
C. Increasing digestion
D. Lowering blood pressure

Answer: A — Generating heat

🧊 COLD TAKE: Shivering converts muscle activity into heat.

5. Which tissue can produce heat without shivering?

B. White blood cells
C. Skin cells
D. Tendons

Answer: A — Brown fat

🧊 COLD TAKE: Brown fat can generate heat through a process called non-shivering thermogenesis.

B. Deep fat
C. Thermal fat
D. Muscle fat

Answer: A — Brown fat

🧊 COLD TAKE: Brown fat contains many mitochondria that help produce heat.

🧊 COLD TAKE: Sudden cold can trigger rapid, involuntary breathing.

🧊 COLD TAKE: Cold shock can rapidly affect breathing, heart rate and blood pressure.

🧊 COLD TAKE: Your autonomic nervous system controls many involuntary responses to cold.
