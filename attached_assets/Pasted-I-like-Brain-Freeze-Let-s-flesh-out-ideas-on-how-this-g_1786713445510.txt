I really like Brain Freeze for this. And I think your instinct about 30-second questions is right. It makes the plunge itself the pacing mechanism, rather than trying to cram a conventional trivia game into a cold plunge.

The important distinction I'd make is:

Trivia answers determine your score.
The plunge determines your multiplier.
The benefit goal determines the length of the run.

That gives each system a job.

Where the points become interesting

I'd use three different point components.

1. Trivia points

Simple:

Correct answer = +100

Maybe difficulty modifies it:

Difficulty	Points
Easy	75
Medium	100
Hard	150
Expert	200

But that's only the base score.

2. Cold Multiplier

This is where your existing benefit system becomes the game mechanic.

Suppose the user's goal is Mood and their target is 2 minutes.

Their multiplier progresses as they reach each benefit stage:

0:00 → ×1
Energy → ×1.5
Mood → ×2
Metabolism → ×2.5
Recovery → ×3

So someone who gets every question right isn't necessarily the highest scorer.

Someone who keeps playing deeper into their plunge earns a larger multiplier.

But there's an important distinction:

The multiplier is earned by reaching the benefit stage, not by simply sitting there longer than the recommended goal.

So if Mood is their goal:

2:00 → ×2 unlocked

If they choose to continue:

3:00 → ×2.5

5:00 → ×3

That makes continuing attractive without making it necessary.

3. Streak bonus

This is where I think Brain Freeze gets addictive.

Every correct answer increases your Brain Streak.

For example:

1 correct → +0%
2 → +10%
3 → +20%
4 → +30%
5+ → +50%

So imagine:

Question 1

100 points

Question 2

100 × 1.1 = 110

Question 3

100 × 1.2 = 120

Question 4

150 × 1.3 = 195

Now you've got:

🔥 STREAK ×4

And you're sitting there thinking:

"Fuck, I don't want to miss the next one."

That's the psychological hook.

But what happens when you get one wrong?

Don't destroy everything.

I'd make it:

❌ INCORRECT
Answer: Jupiter

Streak broken

Your streak multiplier resets.

Your plunge multiplier doesn't.

That's extremely important.

You haven't lost the progress you've earned from actually plunging.

You just lost your trivia combo.

So:

Cold progress = permanent during the run.

Trivia streak = fragile.

That creates tension without making the plunge itself feel like failure.

Personalized
