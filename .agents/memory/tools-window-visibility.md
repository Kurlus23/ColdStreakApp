---
name: Music and Brain Freeze utility window
description: Visibility and walkthrough rules for the shared Music and Brain Freeze controls.
---

The shared Music and Brain Freeze utility window is minimized for new users, with the compact Music control beside the ColdStreak logo. Its expanded state may be remembered after the user chooses it.

**Why:** Music should not compete with the plunge timer, while Brain Freeze must remain discoverable even when its opt-in preference is off.

**How to apply:** Keep the Brain Freeze icon visible in the expanded utility window regardless of preference; an off icon opens opt-in and must never turn trivia on by itself. The first-open walkthrough should open the window while introducing both tools, then restore the minimized default when the tour ends.
