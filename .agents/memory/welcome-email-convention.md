---
name: Approved welcome email
description: Product decision for the canonical ColdStreak new-user welcome email.
---

The current ColdStreak welcome email is approved as the default going forward. If ColdStreak adds or changes a feature, onboarding flow, navigation entry point, or product promise mentioned in the email, remind the product owner to review the welcome email before changing it.

**Why:** The welcome email now has an intentional story: first plunge, tracking, Brain Freeze during and outside plunges, personal insights, community, and streak habit. Uncoordinated copy changes can make that story stale or reorder the primary onboarding action.

**How to apply:** Preserve the approved structure and palette for routine sends. Before editing the template, identify whether the change affects any of its claims or calls to action and explicitly surface a welcome-email review reminder.
