## Rule

Pause the Brain Freeze schedule while the app is backgrounded. Do not stack or rapidly replay missed questions on return. Resume normal question spacing from where the Brain Freeze clock stopped; if the plunge has ended, do not backfill questions afterward.

**Why:** A backlog feels punitive and asks the user to rush through a cognitive game after returning. Pausing preserves the intended rhythm without turning missed screen time into a disadvantage.

**How to apply:** Any Brain Freeze timing or resume change must preserve the per-session question ordering and normal minimum gap, including the every-third cold-plunge slot rule. Treat the plunge timer and Brain Freeze schedule as separate clocks.
