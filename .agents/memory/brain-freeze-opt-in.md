---
name: Brain Freeze opt-in
description: Product decision for how new and existing users enter the in-plunge trivia experience.
---

New accounts must make an explicit one-time choice before Brain Freeze can run: “Turn it on” enables in-plunge trivia, while “Not now” leaves it disabled. Existing accounts retain their current setting and must not be silently switched off.

**Why:** Production behavior showed a high share of zero-result sessions when trivia was automatically enabled, suggesting unanswered questions can feel imposed rather than optional.

**How to apply:** Preserve the choice across devices as an account preference. Any onboarding, registration, or preference migration must keep Brain Freeze disabled for a new account until the choice is resolved, without prompting established accounts merely because they never touched the toggle.
