---
name: Insights consolidation
description: Product rules for avoiding duplicate recovery insights and handling early Brain Freeze data.
---

Profile Insights should present one detailed report for post-plunge check-ins and one clearly labeled monthly check-in trend. Do not add separate all-time “How You Feel,” “Cold Adaptation Trend,” or second monthly sections that repeat the same ratings.

**Why:** Multiple views of the same mood, energy, recovery, and focus data looked like different features with conflicting unlock requirements.

**How to apply:** Put short-window and all-time detail inside the report, reserve the monthly card for month-to-month comparison, and avoid duplicate locked sections.

Show a user their own Brain Freeze accuracy and speed as soon as recorded answers exist. Higher sample thresholds may still gate comparisons, adaptation claims, and emailed conclusions.

**Why:** Several completed sessions could look locked when one context had not reached the comparison threshold, and stale account-loading timing could leave valid data unfetched.

**How to apply:** Re-fetch when the active account changes, display available contexts independently, and explain thresholds only for conclusions that truly require them.
